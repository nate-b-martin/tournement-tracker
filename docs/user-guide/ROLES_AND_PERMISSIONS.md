# Roles & Permissions (End-User Guide)

## Overview

The tournament tracker has four user roles. Your role determines what you can see and do. Sign in to discover your role — the app tells you when you're in read-only mode.

---

---

## Admin

**You are the system administrator.** Full access to everything.

What you can do:
- Create, edit, and delete tournaments, teams, players, games, fields
- Schedule games and enter scores
- Import teams and players
- Change other users' roles
- See and use all edit/delete buttons throughout the app

**Getting started**: You were automatically made admin because you were the first user to sign up. Navigate to **Tournaments** to create your first tournament.

---

## Organizer

**You manage tournaments on the ground.** Tournament-level write access.

What you can do:
- Create new tournaments
- Edit tournament details (dates, bracket type, fields)
- Schedule games and assign fields
- Enter game scores and mark games complete
- Add teams and players to tournaments
- Manage field availability

**Note**: You cannot delete tournaments, delete teams/players, or change user roles. For those, contact an admin.

---

## Player

**You participate in tournaments.** Team-level write access.

What you can do:
- Create and manage teams
- Add players to your team roster
- View your team's schedule and results
- View tournament brackets and standings

**Note**: You cannot create tournaments, schedule games, or change field settings. Contact an organizer or admin to set up tournaments.

---

## Spectator

**You browse tournaments.** Read-only access to everything.

What you can do:
- View all tournaments, teams, players, and games
- See brackets and standings
- Browse player statistics

You **cannot** create, edit, or delete anything. The app shows a yellow banner reminding you that you're in read-only mode.

**Not signed in?** You're effectively a spectator — all public pages are visible with no sign-in required.

---

## How to Know Your Role

After signing in:

1. Open the **sidebar navigation** (hamburger menu top-left)
2. Look at the bottom of the sidebar — your name/email is shown
3. On the **Dashboard**, the stat cards and tables are visible
4. If you see a **yellow banner**: *"Viewing as spectator. Contact an admin to make changes"* — you need higher permissions

## Need a Role Change?

Contact an **Admin** to have your role upgraded. Only admins can change user roles.
